Retail is loud.

Log in and the game hands you fifty things at once. None of them says which one matters this week, so most evenings end with a few world quests and the feeling of running in place.

“I feel dumb in retail. I don't see goals, only grinding.”

A returning player, October 2026

In August 2026 a thread on r/wow about not knowing what to do drew 613 upvotes and 308 comments.

Walk one road.

Slow Road shows your character the next three steps of one road. Nothing else is on the screen.

Finish all three and you make camp. The next stage opens at the weekly reset.

Every week starts with what you did.

On your first visit after a reset, Slow Road shows a short recap. It never lists what you missed.
